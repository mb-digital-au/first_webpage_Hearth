# 🎯 Lead Filtering & Funnel Optimization Strategy

### Project Overview
A comprehensive strategy designed to refine incoming leads, reduce acquisition costs, and maximize conversion rates using automated filtering criteria.

### Key Pillars
* **Audience Segmentation:** Categorizing leads based on high-intent behaviors and industry-specific criteria.
* **Lead Qualification Scoring:** Deploying behavioral filters to ensure sales teams focus strictly on high-value prospects.
* **Funnel Mapping:** Optimizing landing page friction to filter out low-quality entries while maintaining a seamless user journey.

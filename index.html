What Hearth is

A place to talk, with someone who's actually there to listen.

Hearth is a human listening service. When you reach out for a conversation, you're matched with a real, trained listener who gives you their full attention — by phone or video call — for as long as you talk. There's no clinical assessment, no notes filed away, no treatment plan. Just a private conversation, on your terms.

  • A real person, not a script Every conversation is with a human listener — attentive, warm, and present.
  • Private and confidential What you share stays between you and your listener.
  • No judgment, no agenda You don't need a reason. You can talk about anything, or nothing in particular.
  • On your schedule Reach out for a single conversation or a regular check-in — whatever suits you.
Who it's for

For anyone who wants to feel a little less alone.

Hearth is for people of any age or background. You don't need a crisis or a diagnosis to want to talk — sometimes you just do.

Why it matters

Why talking to a real person still matters.

  • Being heard changes how a moment feels Saying something out loud to someone who's genuinely listening can loosen a thought that's been stuck for days.
  • Human attention is different A real listener notices tone, pace, and pauses — and responds to you, not to a pattern.
  • Connection is the point Sometimes the value isn't a solution. It's simply not carrying something alone for a while.
  • No performance required You don't have to explain yourself perfectly. A good listener meets you wherever you're starting from.

Please read: what Hearth is not

Hearth is a human listening service focused on connection and conversation. It is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, or a substitute for professional mental health care. Listeners are not clinicians and cannot diagnose or treat any condition.

Hearth is also not a crisis support service. If you are in crisis, thinking about harming yourself, or in immediate danger, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.

markdown# 📊 Meta Ads Campaign Performance Analysis

### Project Overview
An analytical teardown of Facebook and Instagram ad account metrics, focusing on structural adjustments to scale return on ad spend (ROAS).

### Key Metrics Tracked
* **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Tracking creative resonance across diverse audience buckets.
* **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** Adjusting lookalike audiences to systematically lower structural costs.
* **ROAS Optimization:** Isolating top-performing visual placements to scale budget allocation safely.

markdown# 📸 Social Brand Architecture: Instagram Audit

### Project Overview
A strategic evaluation of visual layout, messaging alignment, and audience interaction patterns to elevate organic visibility.

### Core Strategic Focus
* **Brand Consistency:** Aligning thematic colors, imagery, and messaging across the entire grid.
* **Engagement Engine:** Building narrative-driven captions that encourage shares, saves, and conversions.
* **Bio Strategy:** Transforming standard profiles into clear marketing landing pages with distinct action buttons.
